Moonlight WebRTC

Connect to your PC

The PC must run Sunshine-Web-RTC with its WebRTC server enabled.

Your browser blocked the connection to the PC. This page is served over https, and Sunshine-Web-RTC's connection is not encrypted (ws://). Browsers only allow that from https pages after you say so:

  1. Click the icon to the left of the address bar and open Site settings.
  2. Set Insecure content to Allow.
  3. Come back to this page and select Connect again.

That works in Chrome and Edge, also for the installed app. You can instead run the client yourself over http.
